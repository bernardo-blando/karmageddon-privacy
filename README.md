# Privacy Policy for Karmageddon

**Last Updated**: November 19, 2025

## 1. Introduction

Karmageddon ("we", "our", or "the extension") is a Chrome extension that helps FACEIT players rate and remember teammates and opponents.

## 2. What Data We Handle (Local Storage Only)

**We do NOT collect or transmit any data.** All data is created by you and stored only on your device.

The extension stores the following locally using Chrome's storage API:
- **FACEIT Player Information**: Player nicknames and FACEIT player IDs (from FACEIT's public API)
- **Your Ratings**: Numerical ratings (1-5 scale) you assign to players
- **Rating Tags**: Descriptive tags you select for players (such as "Toxic", "Leader", "Clutch", etc.)
- **Your FACEIT Profile**: Your FACEIT username and player ID (for identification)
- **Timestamps**: When ratings were created or modified

**Important**: This data is created by you, stored only on your device, and never leaves your computer.

## 3. How We Use This Data

- All data remains **locally on your device** using Chrome's secure storage API
- We **do NOT collect** any data
- We **do NOT transmit** any data to external servers
- We **do NOT access** your data remotely
- We **do NOT share** your data with anyone
- Data is used solely to display your ratings when you view FACEIT match rooms and player profiles

## 4. Data Storage and Security

- All data remains on your local device
- No remote servers or databases are used
- Data persists until you manually delete it or uninstall the extension
- Your data is never transmitted over the internet
- We have no ability to access, view, or retrieve your data

## 5. Your Rights and Controls

You have complete control over your data:

- **Export**: You can export all your data at any time via the extension popup
- **Delete**: You can delete individual ratings or all data at any time
- **Disable**: You can disable the extension via the toggle in the popup
- **Uninstall**: Uninstalling the extension automatically removes all stored data

## 6. Data Retention

- Data is retained indefinitely on your device until you choose to delete it
- Uninstalling the extension automatically removes all stored data
- We do not retain any data because we never receive it in the first place

## 7. Third-Party Services

This extension makes API calls to FACEIT's public API to retrieve player information (names, IDs) for display purposes only.

- API calls are made **directly from your browser** to FACEIT's servers
- We do NOT collect, proxy, or store any responses from FACEIT's API
- We do NOT have access to any data from these API calls
- The extension simply displays information that FACEIT already makes publicly available
- Please review [FACEIT's Privacy Policy](https://corporate.faceit.com/privacy/)

**Important**: We never see or collect this data - it goes directly between your browser and FACEIT.

## 8. No Tracking or Analytics

- We do NOT use any analytics services
- We do NOT track your usage or behavior
- We do NOT collect telemetry or diagnostic data
- We do NOT use cookies or tracking technologies
- We do NOT monitor which players you rate or how you use the extension

## 9. Children's Privacy

This extension is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.
